# Bubble Sharks — brand kit

**Name:** Bubble Sharks  **Domain:** bubblesharks.com
**Tagline:** Big fun, little prices.
**Secondary line:** Toys that make bath time, play time and bedtime a splash.

## Positioning
A cheerful, parent-trusted toy shop for kids aged 3–12. The shark is friendly,
goofy and blowing bubbles, never scary. We sell fun first, but every product
page says who it is for (age grade) and what it is made of.

## Voice
- Playful but plain. Short sentences. One joke per page at most.
- Talk to the parent, wink at the kid. "Keeps a 6-year-old busy for an hour.
  Keeps you out of the bath splash zone."
- Always honest about shipping times and sizes.
- Never use "cheap". Use "little prices", "pocket-money friendly".

## Colours
| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ocean | `#0E6BA8` | Buttons, links, header text |
| Bubble | `#7FD1F5` | Backgrounds, badges |
| Foam | `#F4FBFF` | Page background |
| Coral | `#FF6B57` | Sale tags, primary CTA hover |
| Sand | `#FFD166` | Accents, star ratings |
| Deep | `#0B2545` | Body text, footer |

Contrast: Ocean on Foam and Deep on Bubble both pass WCAG AA for text.

## Type
- Headings: **Fredoka** (Google Fonts), bold, slightly rounded.
- Body: **Nunito**, regular 16px, line height 1.6.
- Both are available in the Shopify theme font picker.

## Logo brief
Rounded blue shark, side view, eyes wide and friendly, three bubbles rising
from its mouth, wordmark "Bubble Sharks" in Fredoka Bold underneath in Deep.
Deliver as SVG plus 512px PNG on transparent, and a square favicon of just the
shark head. Claude can generate a first draft once image tools are available,
or commission one on Fiverr for about $30–60.

## Photography
Bright, white or Foam backgrounds, toy in use by a child's hands, no faces
needed. Supplier photos are fine for launch; replace hero images with
lifestyle shots in month two.
