# Hi, we're Bubble Sharks

We started Bubble Sharks because toy shopping had become a chore: endless
scrolling, mystery brands, and a cart full of things that break by Tuesday.

So we keep it small on purpose. Every toy in the shop is picked by hand, checked
for an honest age grade and safe materials, and priced so that a birthday or a
rainy-afternoon treat does not need a spreadsheet.

**What we promise**

- Clear age ranges on every product, so the gift fits the kid.
- Honest delivery times, shown before you pay.
- Free shipping on orders over $50, and easy returns within 30 days.
- A real person answering support@bubblesharks.com within one business day.

Thanks for letting us be part of the fun.
