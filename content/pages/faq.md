# Frequently asked questions

**How long does delivery take?**
Most orders arrive in 5–12 business days. The exact estimate is shown on each
product page and again at checkout. You get a tracking link by email the moment
your parcel ships.

**Where do you ship?**
United States for now. Join the newsletter to hear when we open more countries.

**Are the toys safe?**
Every product lists its age grade and materials. We do not list items with small
parts for children under 3, and we only source from suppliers who provide
safety documentation for the US market (CPSIA / ASTM F963).

**Can I return something?**
Yes, within 30 days of delivery, unused and in its original packaging. See our
Refund policy for the steps.

**Do you have a physical shop?**
No, we are online only, which is how we keep prices low.

**How do I contact you?**
Email support@bubblesharks.com or use the Contact page. We reply within one
business day.
