# Privacy policy

Bubble Sharks ("we", "us") operates bubblesharks.com. This policy explains what
personal information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

## What we collect
- **Order information:** name, billing and shipping address, email, phone,
  payment details (processed by our payment provider; we never store full card
  numbers).
- **Device information:** browser, IP address, pages viewed, and cookies, used
  to run the site, prevent fraud and understand what customers look at.
- **Marketing preferences:** your email and consent if you subscribe.

## How we use it
To fulfil orders, send order updates, provide support, prevent fraud, improve
the store, and, with your consent, send marketing email you can unsubscribe
from at any time.

## Who we share it with
Shopify (hosting and checkout), our payment providers, our fulfilment partners
who ship your order, email and analytics providers, and authorities when the
law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

## Your rights
You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by
emailing support@bubblesharks.com. California residents have additional rights
under the CCPA, and EU/UK residents under the GDPR.

## Children
Our store sells toys for children but is intended for use by adults. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

## Changes
We will post updates here. Last updated: launch date.
