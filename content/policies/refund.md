# Refund policy

We accept returns within **30 days of delivery** for items that are unused,
in their original packaging and in resalable condition.

## How to return
1. Email support@bubblesharks.com with your order number and the item.
2. We reply within one business day with the return address and instructions.
3. Send the item back. You cover return postage unless the item arrived
   damaged, faulty or not as described, in which case we send a prepaid label.

## Refunds
Once we receive and inspect the return we refund the item price to your
original payment method within 5 business days. Original shipping fees are not
refunded unless the return is our fault.

## Damaged or wrong items
Tell us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we will replace it or refund
you in full, no return needed in most cases.

## Not returnable
Items marked final sale, gift cards, and opened hygiene-sensitive items such as
bath toys that have been used.
