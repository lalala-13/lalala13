# Shipping policy

**Where:** We currently ship within the United States.

**Cost:** Free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 ship for a flat $4.95.

**Processing:** Orders are processed within 1–3 business days.

**Delivery:** Most parcels arrive 5–12 business days after dispatch. The
estimate shown on each product page and at checkout is specific to that item.
Some items ship separately from different warehouses and may arrive on
different days; you pay shipping only once.

**Tracking:** A tracking link is emailed when your order ships.

**Delays:** Customs, weather and carrier issues can add a few days. If a parcel
has not moved for 10 business days, email support@bubblesharks.com and we will
resend or refund.

**Address errors:** Please check your address at checkout. We cannot redirect a
parcel after dispatch, and returned-to-sender parcels are refunded minus
shipping.
