# Product sourcing rules (toys)

Used when picking launch products in the supplier app. A product goes in only
if every "must" passes.

## Must
- Age grade stated by supplier and matches our 3–12 range. Nothing graded 0–3
  that has small parts, magnets, button batteries or strings over 22 cm.
- Supplier provides US safety documentation on request (CPSIA / ASTM F963
  test report, tracking label). Keep a note per product.
- Ships to US within 12 business days on the default shipping method, with
  tracking.
- Supplier rating ≥ 4.5 and ≥ 100 orders, or a Zendrop/CJ "verified" badge.
- No licensed characters (Disney, Marvel, Pokémon, Paw Patrol, etc.) and no
  brand look-alikes. No weapons-styled toys beyond water blasters.
- At least 4 usable photos, one on a plain background.

## Pricing
- Retail = landed cost (product + shipping to customer) × 2.5 to 3, rounded to
  x.95 or x.99. Minimum retail $9.95. Floor margin $6 per unit after fees.
- Compare-at price only when the supplier's own list price supports it.
- Free-shipping threshold $50; target average order value $38.

| Collection | SKUs | Notes |

| Sensory & Fidget | 5–6 | Cheapest tier, under $15 |
| Games & Puzzles | 4–6 | Family gifting |

## Listing rewrite rules
- Title: `<What it is> for <who>` under 60 chars, e.g. "Bubble Shark Bath Squirter Set for Toddlers".
- First line of description: who it is for and what it does, one sentence.
- Bullets: 3–5 benefits, then "Age & safety" (age grade, materials, supervision
  note), then "In the box", then "Shipping" (processing + delivery estimate).
- Tags: one of `bath|outdoor|stem|plush|sensory|games`, one of
  `age-3-5|age-6-8|age-9-12`, plus `new` on launch and `bestseller` once an
  item has 10+ orders.
